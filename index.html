DocKeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

DocKeeper ("the app") is a private vault for keeping personal and family documents on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your documents stay on your phone, and we do not run any server that receives your data.

Who we are

DocKeeper is published by Deepak Kumar. Contact: vasu.deepak12@gmail.com.

What the app stores

  • Documents you add (photos, scans and PDFs), and details you type about them, such as names, dates, notes and tags.
  • The people (profiles) you create to organise documents.
  • Your settings, such as theme and reminder days.

All of this is stored on your phone only. Files and document details are encrypted (AES-256), and the encryption key is kept in the phone's secure keystore. We cannot see your documents, and we do not collect them.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app signs in with your Google account and saves an encrypted backup file in the private app-data area of your own Google Drive. Only DocKeeper can access that area, and it is not visible in your normal Drive files. The app uses your Google account email address only to show which account is connected. We do not receive your email address, your backup or any of your files; the transfer is directly between your phone and your Google Drive. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account and Drive.

Backup is off unless you turn it on. You can disconnect at any time in Settings.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only to scan documents when you choose to.
  • Photos / files: only the images or PDFs you pick to add.
  • Notifications: to remind you before a document expires. Reminders are created on your phone; no server is involved.
  • Biometrics / fingerprint: optional, to unlock the app. Your fingerprint or face data is handled by Android and is never given to the app.
  • Internet: only for the optional Google Drive backup and for Google Play features such as update checks and the rating prompt.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking of your use of the app.
  • We do not sell or share your information.
  • We do not send your documents or details to any server run by us.

Google Play services

The app may use Google Play features (checking for app updates and asking for a rating). These are provided by Google and are covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a document, a person, or everything in the app, and uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. A backup in your Google Drive stays until you remove it: disconnect and delete it from Google Drive settings (Manage apps, DocKeeper, Delete hidden app data), or erase it through your Google account. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it for you if you lose your phone and have no backup.

Security

Files and details are encrypted on the device, and the app can be protected with a PIN and biometrics. No method is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked and your PIN private. If you forget your PIN and your security answer, the only way back in is to erase the app's data.

Children

DocKeeper is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.
