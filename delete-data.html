Delete your data

DocKeeper: Document Vault · by Deepak Kumar

DocKeeper keeps your documents on your phone. We do not run a server and we do not hold a copy of your data, so you can delete everything yourself. These are the steps.

1. Delete data inside the app

  • One document: open it, then tap Delete.
  • One person and all their documents: on the home screen, press and hold the person, tap the bin and confirm.
  • Everything: delete every person as above, or uninstall the app (step 3).

What is deleted: the encrypted document files, the details you typed (names, dates, notes, tags), the people you created and the app settings.

2. Delete your Google Drive backup (only if you turned backup on)

  • Open Google Drive on a computer: drive.google.com/drive/settings.
  • Click Manage apps, find DocKeeper, then choose Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • You can also use Options → Disconnect from Drive, and remove DocKeeper's access in your Google Account permissions.

What is deleted: the encrypted backup file that DocKeeper saved in your own Drive.

3. Uninstall the app

Removing DocKeeper from your phone deletes all of its data on that phone.

What we keep

Nothing. We never receive your documents, your details or your backup, so there is nothing for us to keep and no retention period. Deleting the data on your phone and in your Google Drive removes it completely. Because there is no copy with us, deleted data cannot be recovered.
